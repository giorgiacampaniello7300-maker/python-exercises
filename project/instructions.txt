Your goal is to get all the necessary ingredients to create a traditional Italian wedding menu to save the wedding.

Commands:
- "Explore": walk and look around to find ingredients and supplies
- "Travel": move to the next city after completing your tasks
- "Collect": collect the cooler bag in Milan
- "Use": keep, exchange or buy cheese in Rome
- "Inventory": see all the items you have collected
- "Cook": cook the food for the guests
- "Save": save the current location, inventory and general progress

When the game starts, choose "New" to begin a new game or "Continue" to load your saved game. 
Type "lopeta" to end the game.
Save before typing "lopeta", because quitting doesn't save the game automatically.
Pay attention to capital letters: type commands exactly as shown.
