Your goal is to get all the necessary ingredients to create a traditional Italian wedding menu to save the wedding.

Commands:
- "Explore": walk and look around to find ingredients and supplies, change city
- "Eat": restore your energy
- "Collect": collect items while you explore the cities
- "Use": use an item you collected or exchange it (exchange option not available yet)
- "Inventory": see all the items you have collected
- "Save": save the progress and continue later

Pay attention to capital letters. Type commands and item names exactly as shown.
Type "lopeta" to end the game.
